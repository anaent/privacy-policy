2. Information we collect

When you sign in with Facebook and use the Service, we access and process the following, limited to the permissions you grant:

  • Basic profile — your Facebook user ID and name, via Facebook Login (public_profile).
  • Pages you manage — the list of Facebook Pages administered by your account (pages_show_list), used solely so you can select the Page connected to your brand's Instagram business account.
  • Page engagement metrics — impressions, engaged users, post engagements, and reach of the Page you select (pages_read_engagement), used to measure campaign performance.
  • Instagram business account linkage — the Instagram business account connected to your Page (instagram_basic, business_management).
  • Creator Marketplace data — creator profiles returned by the Instagram Creator Marketplace Discovery API (instagram_creator_marketplace_discovery), such as username, biography, follower counts, reach, engagement rates, audience demographics, and (where the creator has made it available) a business contact email or portfolio URL.

We do not collect your Facebook password, private messages, friends list, or any data unrelated to the features above.

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate you and connect your Facebook Page and Instagram business account.
  • Search and evaluate Instagram creators for partnership-advertising campaigns.
  • Maintain an internal shortlist of candidate creators for sponsorship review.
  • Measure engagement and performance of campaigns on your connected Facebook Page.

We do not use Platform Data for advertising targeting, profiling unrelated to the Service, or any purpose other than those listed above. We do not sell personal data.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Data accessed through the Service is used within A&A ENT Inc. for the sourcing and operations team's campaign work. We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer Meta Platform Data or your personal data to third parties, except:

  • Service providers acting solely as infrastructure for us (cloud hosting and database hosting), bound to process data only on our instructions.
  • Where required by applicable law or lawful government request.

In the past 12 months we have received no national-security data requests.

5. Data retention

OAuth access tokens are held only for the duration of your session in the browser and are not stored on our servers. Creator and campaign data retrieved through the APIs is retained only while your account or the relevant campaign is active, and is deleted or anonymized within 90 days of account closure or a deletion request.

6. Data deletion

You may request deletion of your data at any time:

  • Follow the instructions on our Data Deletion page, or
  • Email kw@mightyfine.co.kr with the subject "Data Deletion Request".

You can also revoke the Service's access at any time from your Facebook Apps and Websites settings; after revoking, email kw@mightyfine.co.kr and we will remove any remaining associated data.

7. Security

We apply access controls and encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) to all Service endpoints. Access to creator and campaign data is restricted to authorized A&A personnel.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise any of these rights, contact kw@mightyfine.co.kr.

9. Children

The Service is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
