# Privacy Policy — CreatorHub & CreatorHub Collector

**Effective Date:** October 11, 2026  
**Entity:** A&A ENT Inc. (support@ana-ent.com)

---

## 1. Overview & Single Purpose
This Privacy Policy explains how **A&A ENT Inc.** ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the **CreatorHub** platform and the **CreatorHub Collector** Chrome Extension.

The single core purpose of **CreatorHub Collector** is to enable authorized operators to collect publicly displayed creator metrics (such as subscriber/follower counts, public video view statistics, engagement rates, and aggregated demographic summaries) across supported social platforms (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Naver Blog/Clip, and Meta Business Suite) and synchronize them into their organization's CreatorHub influencer marketing campaign database.

---

## 2. Information We Do and Do Not Collect

### What We Collect:
- **Public Website Content (Creator Channel Metrics):** When an operator visits a creator's public profile on a supported platform, the extension reads publicly visible channel metrics (e.g., channel name, handle, subscriber count, recent video view counts, public contact email if provided in the bio) and aggregated audience demographics.
- **Local Operational Preferences:** The extension uses local client storage (chrome.storage.local) exclusively to remember the operator's floating panel placement on the screen and temporarily cache active campaign lists (5-minute TTL) to minimize redundant network requests.

### What We Do NOT Collect:
- **No Personal User Data:** We do NOT collect personal information of the extension user/operator (no personal identity numbers, personal financial details, health data, or personal browsing history).
- **No Keystroke or Activity Tracking:** We do NOT log general browsing history, search queries, passwords, or unrelated web activity.
- **No Third-Party Advertising Trackers:** The extension contains no analytics trackers, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising SDKs.

---

## 3. How We Use and Share Information
- **Purpose Limitation:** Collected public creator data is used strictly and exclusively to evaluate and manage influencer marketing campaigns within the CreatorHub system.
- **No Data Sales:** We do **never sell, rent, or lease** any collected data to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers.
- **No Lending or Credit Scoring:** Collected data is never used to determine creditworthiness or for any lending/financial underwriting purposes.
- **Secure Transmission:** All data exchanged between the extension and CreatorHub servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.

---

## 4. Manifest V3 & Remote Code Compliance
The extension strictly adheres to Google Chrome Web Store Manifest V3 requirements:
- All executable code is packaged locally within the published extension package.
- No remote scripts, external code loaders, or eval() execution are utilized.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion
Collected creator profile data is stored securely in CreatorHub database systems. Operators and creators may request review or deletion of their channel profile records at any time by contacting our privacy team.
